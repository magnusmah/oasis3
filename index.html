About This Property

This is a rare 4-storey freehold shop office at Oasis 3 @ Tropicana Danga Cove, located along the main road in Taman Cahaya Kota Puteri, Masai — one of Johor Bahru's fastest-growing commercial corridors.

The building sits on a 24 × 70 ft (1,680 sqft) freehold land with 6,720 sqft of total built-up across 4 floors. A private passenger lift services all floors, making upper levels fully functional for business use. The main road frontage provides high visibility and easy vehicle access.

The property is sold with an existing tenancy, providing the buyer with immediate rental income and a 4.4% annual yield from day one. This makes it suitable as a passive income investment or as a long-term capital appreciation hold in an appreciating commercial area.

Strategically located near the Eastern Dispersal Link (EDL), East Coast Highway, and Pasir Gudang Highway — 15 minutes to Johor Bahru City Centre CIQ Complex, 13 minutes to Pasir Gudang, and 35 minutes to Senai International Airport.
